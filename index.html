Who We Are

Eyes of BC is a British Columbia-based, medical student-led community initiative dedicated to promoting eye health, improving awareness, and addressing barriers to vision care. We believe everyone deserves access to reliable eye-health information, regardless of where they live, the language they speak, or their financial and social circumstances. Through community outreach, educational initiatives, and partnerships, we hope to support communities that may face greater challenges accessing eye care.

Mission, Vision & Values

Our Work

Eyes of BC works with students, healthcare professionals, and community organizations to promote eye-health awareness across British Columbia. Our outreach focuses on practical education, community engagement, and reaching populations that may face additional barriers to accessing eye care.

Rural Eye Health Outreach

Accessing eye care can be challenging in rural and remote communities, where residents may need to travel considerable distances. Eyes of BC has undertaken outreach in northern British Columbia, including Tumbler Ridge, Chetwynd, Fort St. John, and Dawson Creek. Sessions cover common eye conditions, warning signs requiring medical attention, and appropriate eye examinations.

Newcomer & Refugee Eye Health Education

Eyes of BC has collaborated with community organizations serving newcomers and refugees to deliver eye-health education covering common eye conditions, prevention, recognizing concerning symptoms, and where to seek care. Planned: multilingual materials and navigation resources.

Children & Family Eye Health

Interactive activities and family events. Topics: how our eyes work; common vision problems in children; myopia and healthy visual habits; amblyopia and strabismus; signs of possible vision problems; when to seek professional eye care.

Community Education & Outreach

Sessions adapted for seniors, families, newcomer communities, and people with limited access to health information. Topics: cataracts; glaucoma; age-related macular degeneration; diabetic retinopathy; dry eye; eye injuries and urgent symptoms; children's vision; preventive eye care.

Hoop for Sight

In 2026, Hoop for Sight raised approximately $13,000 in support of Orbis.
