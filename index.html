About Eyes of BC

Eyes of BC is a British Columbia-based, medical student-led community initiative dedicated to promoting eye health, improving awareness, and addressing barriers to vision care. We believe everyone deserves access to reliable eye-health information, regardless of where they live, the language they speak, or their financial and social circumstances. Through community outreach, educational initiatives, and partnerships, we hope to support communities that may face greater challenges accessing eye care.

What guides us

Mission, Vision & Values

Mission

Empowering communities through accessible education and supporting equitable access to vision care.

Vision

A British Columbia where everyone has the knowledge, resources, and opportunity to protect their vision, regardless of their background or circumstances.

Education

sharing accurate, accessible, practical eye-health information

Equity

communities face different barriers and solutions must respond to their needs

Community

working alongside local organizations, healthcare professionals, and people with lived experience

Collaboration

partnerships that support sustainable, community-informed initiatives

Empowerment

helping individuals make informed decisions about their eye health and understand care options

Looking ahead

resource navigation; community partnerships; rural eye care access; understanding barriers to care; medical student education.
