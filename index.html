Why Eye Health Matters in Canada

1.2 million

Canadians estimated to live with significant vision loss or blindness

Source: Health Canada, National Strategy for Eye Care (2026)
8+ million

More than 8 million estimated to have AMD, cataract, diabetic retinopathy or glaucoma

Source: Health Canada, National Strategy for Eye Care (2026)
Up to 40%

Canadians with definite glaucoma in a cited study were unaware

Source: Health Canada, National Strategy for Eye Care (2026)
25%

About 25% of Canadian youth have myopia

Source: Health Canada, National Strategy for Eye Care (2026)

Out in the community

Our Work

Eyes of BC works with students, healthcare professionals, and community organizations to promote eye-health awareness across British Columbia, reaching rural and remote communities, newcomers, children and families, Indigenous communities, and low-income and other underserved communities.

About Eyes of BC

Mission

Empowering communities through accessible education and supporting equitable access to vision care.

Vision

A British Columbia where everyone has the knowledge, resources, and opportunity to protect their vision, regardless of their background or circumstances.

Education

sharing accurate, accessible, practical eye-health information

Equity

communities face different barriers and solutions must respond to their needs

Community

working alongside local organizations, healthcare professionals, and people with lived experience

Collaboration

partnerships that support sustainable, community-informed initiatives

Empowerment

helping individuals make informed decisions about their eye health and understand care options
