Eye Health Equity

1.2 million Canadians estimated to live with significant vision loss or blindness

Source: Health Canada, National Strategy for Eye Care (2026)

More than 8 million estimated to have AMD, cataract, diabetic retinopathy or glaucoma

Source: Health Canada, National Strategy for Eye Care (2026)

By 2050 vision loss or blindness could affect twice as many Canadians

Source: Health Canada, National Strategy for Eye Care (2026)

Up to 40% of Canadians with definite glaucoma in a cited study were unaware

Source: Health Canada, National Strategy for Eye Care (2026)

About 25% of Canadian youth have myopia

Source: Health Canada, National Strategy for Eye Care (2026)

Why gaps exist

travel distance, cost of exams/glasses/medication/transport, language, limited awareness of benefits, difficulty navigating care.

Rural & Remote

limited local professionals, travel, delays, repeat follow-up, low-vision rehab access, weather

What could help

mobile eye care, teleophthalmology, visiting specialist clinics, referral pathways

Indigenous Eye Health

geographic isolation, shortages, cost, transport, systemic discrimination; communities are diverse

What could help

Indigenous-led initiatives, culturally safe care, local/visiting services, clear benefit info, relationships, Indigenous involvement in design

Refugees & Newcomers

language, unfamiliar system, cost, transport, coverage uncertainty

What could help

some eligible refugees and claimants may receive vision benefits through the Interim Federal Health Program (IFHP), eligibility, co-payments, covered services and participating providers must be confirmed

Housing Insecurity

exams, glasses, medication storage, follow-up

What could help

shelter outreach, local optometrist partnerships, low-cost/donated eyewear, navigation

Financial & other barriers

medically necessary physician services publicly insured; routine exams, glasses, contacts vary; transport, language, disability access, appointments, rehab, lack of benefit info

What could help

clear benefit info

Living Well With Vision Loss

rehab, assistive technology, orientation and mobility, accessible materials, workplace accommodations, community support

What could help

rehab, assistive technology, orientation and mobility, accessible materials, workplace accommodations, community support
