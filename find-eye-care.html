Where can I get eye care

Check your coverage

  • Medical Services Plan (BC)

    BC's public health insurance for insured medical services.

  • Income assistance (BC)

    Provincial assistance programs; check whether optical benefits apply to you.

  • Interim Federal Health Program

    Temporary health coverage for eligible refugees and refugee claimants.

  • IFHP coverage summary

    What the program covers.

  • Non-Insured Health Benefits for First Nations and Inuit

    Federal health benefits for eligible First Nations and Inuit.

  • FNHA Health Benefits

    Benefits for First Nations people in BC.

Find and check a provider

  • College of Health and Care Professionals of BC

    Regulator that holds the public register for BC optometrists; use it to check that an optometrist is registered.

  • College of Physicians and Surgeons of BC

    Regulator that holds the public register for BC physicians, including ophthalmologists.

  • Access Map

Living with vision loss

  • CNIB

    Programs and supports for people who are blind or have low vision.

  • HealthLink BC

    Free health advice line and information.
